# ScholarSync 🎓

**Your Student Financial Wellness Platform** — built for HBCU students, by HBCU students.

Built at the Bank of America Code-a-Thon 2026 (North Carolina A&T State University) by Ethan Hall and Miles Johnson.

## The Problem

Financial stress is the #1 driver of student dropout, and it hits HBCU students especially hard:

- **Fragmented ecosystem** — tuition, aid, and expenses live across disconnected portals, so students can't see their full financial picture
- **Information gap** — students miss scholarship deadlines and eligibility requirements because information isn't centralized
- **Underserved by generic tools** — most financial apps don't address the specific needs of HBCU students
- **$100M+ in aid goes unclaimed** every year as a result

## The Solution

ScholarSync is a unified, AI-powered financial hub that brings everything into one dashboard:

- **Campus Innovation** — a centralized view unifying financial, academic, and career resources in one place
- **Funding the Dream** — smart matching connects students to scholarships, aid, and funding they actually qualify for
- **Financial Wellness** — an AI-powered coach guides real-time budgeting and financial decisions
- **Career Exploration** — integrated tools match students to internships and opportunities aligned with their goals
